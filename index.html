Nothing leaves your device.

  • Your files are processed by your own browser, not by a server.
  • Once a tool has loaded, it keeps working with the internet switched off.
  • The source code is public on GitHub, so you can check any of this yourself.

One exception: Audio Transcriber's OpenAI mode sends your audio to OpenAI with your own key. Its offline mode, which is the default, never leaves your device. Most tools also come as a Python app, see the repository README.
