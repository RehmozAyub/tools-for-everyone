AI Text Rewriter

Model

Downloads once, then stays in this browser. Coming back is instant and works with the internet off.

Keep the model

Save it as a file you keep, then load it back later, here or on another machine, with no download at all.

Conversation

Ask for a rewrite, then keep going. Say "make it shorter" or "more formal" and it works on the last reply. The model runs inside your browser, so nothing you write is sent anywhere.

Enter sends, Shift and Enter makes a new line

Model library

Bigger models write better and hold more text at once, and cost a longer download and a slower reply. Each one is kept after its first download.
