What to remove

Some details cannot be kept, such as maker notes, IPTC, preview pictures and XMP history. They are always removed. You can also tick single fields to keep in each file's list.

Phones often save photos sideways with a note that says how to turn them. This keeps only that note, so the photo stays upright.

In Word, Excel, PowerPoint and PDF comments and tracked changes, names become "Author" and initials and dates are removed.

TIFF becomes PNG and HEIC becomes JPEG. These cannot be cleaned without converting, and HEIC photos are re-encoded.

See the hidden details in photos and documents, such as where a photo was taken, which phone took it and who wrote a file, and remove them before you share.

What gets cleaned in each format
JPEG, PNG, WebP
EXIF (location, device, dates, serial numbers), XMP, IPTC, comments, preview pictures and extra data after the picture are removed. The picture itself is copied unchanged, so there is no loss of quality. The colour profile is kept.
PDF
Document info (author, title, dates, software), XMP on the document, pages and pictures, private app data, and EXIF inside embedded photos. Comment authors are anonymised if you choose. Attached files are not cleaned.
DOCX, XLSX, PPTX
Author, last modified by, dates, revision, company, custom properties, the preview picture, comment and tracked-change authors, and metadata in photos inside the file.
TIFF
Can only be cleaned by converting to PNG, in browsers that can open TIFF (such as Safari).
HEIC
Converted to JPEG, then cleaned. The photo is re-encoded, so quality drops slightly.
BMP
Has no place for location, camera or author details, so there is nothing to remove.
