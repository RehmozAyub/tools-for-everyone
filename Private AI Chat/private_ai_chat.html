    Model library

    Bigger models write better, hold more of the conversation, and answer at greater length. They also cost a longer download and a slower reply. Longest reply is how much a model can write in one go before it has to stop, which is the usual reason an answer ends early. Each model is kept after its first download, and Download now fetches one ahead of time, on Wi-Fi for example.

    Everything elseAny model that runs in a browser, not just the few picked here.

    Searching asks huggingface.co for its list of browser ready models, which is the one thing on this page that talks to anyone else apart from the model download itself. It sends what you type in the box and nothing more, never your conversation. None of what comes back has been tried here, so expect some of it to be poor.
